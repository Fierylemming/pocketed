Pocketed privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Pocketed is a personal notes and reminders app. It is run privately by its owner for their own use; it is not a commercial service and has no advertising or analytics.

What Pocketed stores

  • Your notes, reminders, sections and settings.
  • A salted hash of your Pocketed password and recovery key (never the password itself).
  • If you connect Google Calendar: the connection details (a secret calendar link, or a Google sign-in token) and the reminders created from your events.

This is kept in Pocketed's own database (Cloudflare Workers and D1) and in your browser's local storage on the devices you sign in on.

Google Calendar

When you choose Sign in with Google, Pocketed asks for read-only access to your Google Calendar (calendar.readonly). It uses that access only to:

  • read the events in your main calendar for roughly the next two months, and
  • turn each event's title, time, location, description and colour into a reminder in Pocketed.

Pocketed never changes, deletes or creates anything in your Google Calendar, never reads your Google account's other data, and does not sell, share or transfer Google data to anyone. Google data is not used for advertising or to train AI models, and no person reads it.

Pocketed's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • In Pocketed: ⚙️ Settings → Google Calendar → Disconnect removes the imported reminders, deletes the stored Google token and asks Google to revoke Pocketed's access.
  • At any time in your Google account: myaccount.google.com → Third-party connections → Pocketed → remove access.
  • Deleting a note or reminder in Pocketed removes it from every device.
