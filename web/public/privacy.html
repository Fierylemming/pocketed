Pocketed privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Pocketed is a personal notes and reminders app. It is run privately by its owner for their own use; it is not a commercial service and has no advertising or analytics.

What Pocketed stores

  • Your notes, reminders, sections and settings.
  • A salted hash of your Pocketed password and recovery key on the server. Your browser keeps the password on trusted devices so it can sync your notes.
  • If you connect Google Calendar: the connection details (a secret calendar link, or Google sign-in tokens, account identifiers and email addresses for each connected account), account visibility preferences, and the reminders created from your events.
  • If you connect Google Classroom: a Google sign-in token, each connected student account's ID and email, and reminders made from your classes' published assignment/question deadlines.

Your notes, reminders and sections are kept in Pocketed's own database (Cloudflare Workers and D1) and copied to your browser's local storage on trusted devices. Google tokens and secret calendar links are kept only on the server, never in browser storage.

Google Calendar

When you choose Sign in with Google, Pocketed asks for read-only access to your Google Calendar (calendar.readonly) plus openid and email to identify the account. Multiple school/home accounts can stay connected. It uses that access only to:

  • identify only the signed-in account and its email address,
  • read the events in all your readable calendars for roughly the next two months, and
  • turn each event's title, time, location, description and colour into a reminder in Pocketed.

Pocketed never changes, deletes or creates anything in your Google Calendar, never reads your Drive files, schoolwork or other unrelated Google data through the Calendar connection. Google data is not sold, sent to unrelated users, used for advertising or to train AI models, or used for unrelated human review. It is available to the trusted people using your Pocketed workspace.

Pocketed's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Account switches filter only the calendar view and are shared across devices using the same workspace. Turning Calendar events off does not disconnect an account or delete its data: hidden events keep syncing, remain in reminder lists/search, and can still send alerts. Classroom assignments remain visible regardless of the Calendar account switches. Other colour/date filters still apply.

Google Classroom

Connecting Classroom requests classroom.courses.readonly and classroom.coursework.me.readonly, plus openid and email to identify the connected student account. Pocketed uses that access only to:

  • list the active classes the student is enrolled in,
  • read published assignment/question titles, descriptions, deadlines and Classroom links, and
  • create or update reminders in Pocketed's calendar.

Google's coursework permission mentions grades, but Pocketed does not fetch grades, submissions, class rosters, Drive files or attachments. It cannot submit work or change anything in Classroom. School-managed accounts may require administrator approval. A guardian account is not a substitute for connecting the student's own account.

Classroom and Calendar credentials are stored independently in the server's database. Tokens are never sent to the browser or placed in the redirect URL. Imported reminder data is synced to the trusted devices using the same Pocketed workspace password. It is not shared with unrelated users, used for advertising, sold, or used to train AI models. The Google API Services User Data Policy and Limited Use commitments above apply to Classroom data as well.

Removing your data

  • In Pocketed: ⚙️ Settings → Google Calendar → Disconnect account in an account's card removes only that account's Calendar imports and stored credentials. Other accounts and Classroom remain. In Settings → Google Classroom → Disconnect Classroom in a student's card, only that student's Classroom reminders and credentials are removed. Deleting a note/reminder syncs a deletion marker; it is not a secure purge of the underlying sync history, server backups or previously downloaded files.
  • Google can revoke the app's entire grant, not just one feature. Pocketed asks Google to revoke access only when no remaining connection may rely on that account's grant. Unidentified legacy Calendar identities are handled conservatively, so automatic revocation may be deferred rather than break an active connection. You can revoke access directly in each Google account at any time.
  • At any time in your Google account: myaccount.google.com → Third-party connections → Pocketed → remove access.
  • Deleting a note or reminder in Pocketed removes it from every device.
